Tatkal Booking Consistency

Resilient payment–booking consistency for high-demand ticket booking on AWS. The AWS services here are real; the payment gateway and the money are simulated.

The problem

Buying a ticket needs two things to both happen: your money is taken, and a seat is booked. They happen in separate systems, so one can succeed while the other fails, and the customer is left paid with no ticket, or charged twice.

Two systems, same failure

On the left, the system charges first and books second, and nothing repairs a break. On the right, a saga holds the seat, pays once using an idempotency key, confirms, and repairs failures. A reconciler and a dead-letter queue catch the rest.
